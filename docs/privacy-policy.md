# Privacy Policy — Expat8

**Last updated:** 2026-05-14

## 1. Overview

Expat8 ("the app") is a vocabulary learning application. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Device Identifier

We generate a random **device ID** the first time you launch the app. This ID is stored locally on your device and sent to our backend server alongside learning activity requests. It identifies your device session and is not linked to your name, email, or any personally identifiable information.

If you create an account (optional), a **session token** is issued and stored locally. The token expires and is refreshed as you use the app.

### 2.2 Learning Progress

We store your vocabulary learning progress — including which words you have studied, your review history, and study ratings — both **locally on your device** (using an on-device database) and **synced to our backend server** (`expat8.x51.vn`). This data is used to power spaced-repetition scheduling and to restore your progress if you reinstall the app.

### 2.3 Audio Recordings (Speaking Drill Feature)

If you use the **speaking drill** feature, the app requests access to your device microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO` permission). Audio is recorded locally to evaluate your pronunciation. Audio recordings are processed on-device or sent to our server only for the duration of the drill session; they are not stored permanently on the server.

### 2.4 App Usage Events

We collect basic anonymous usage events (e.g., "session started", "word reviewed") to understand how the app is used and improve it. These events do not include the content of your speech or any personally identifiable information.

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect your name, email address, or phone number unless you choose to create an account.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising SDKs.
- We do **not** sell your data to third parties.
- We do **not** use tracking or fingerprinting technologies.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). On-device data is stored in the app's private storage and is not accessible to other apps.

## 5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your server-side learning data by contacting us (see Section 7). On-device data can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

## 6. Children

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: **privacy@x51.vn**

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.
